# View post-meeting summary card

Use the post-meeting summary card to review key takeaways and plan next steps after a customer meeting. In this article, you learn how to:

- Find the post-meeting summary card in Teams.
- Review the insights included on the card.
- Open detailed meeting insights in Sales agent or the meeting recap in Teams.

The card appears after a meeting ends and the recording and transcription are available. Depending on whether you have access to Microsoft 365 Copilot, you see one of two versions of the card.

## View the post-meeting summary card

To view the post-meeting summary card:

1. Open Microsoft Teams.
2. Open your personal chat with the **Sales** bot, or open the **Chat** tab in the **Sales** personal app.
3. Find the card sent after your meeting.

:::image type="content" source="media/post-meeting-summary-card.png" alt-text="Screenshot of a post-meeting summary card with key takeaways and the Review past meeting insights button.":::

The card includes:

- **Meeting details**: The meeting title, date, and time.
- **Key takeaways**: AI-generated highlights of the main discussion points from the meeting.
- **Review past meeting insights**: A button that opens the post-meeting page in [Sales agent in Microsoft 365 Copilot](use-sales-chat.md#prepare-for-and-follow-up-on-your-sales-meetings). On this page, you can review detailed insights from the meeting. The post-meeting experience includes:

    - **Key takeaways** that summarize the most important meeting outcomes.
    - **Meeting summary** and **Action items** from the [existing AI meeting recap](meeting-prep.md#detailed-meeting-preparation-view).

> [!NOTE]
> - If the meeting organizer doesn't grant everyone access to the meeting recording and transcription, the post-meeting summary card isn't generated.
> - The card is generated after the recording and transcription are processed. You receive a notification in Teams when the card is ready.

## Alternate version of the post-meeting summary card

If you don't have access to Microsoft 365 Copilot, the post-meeting summary card appears in the alternate format shown in the following image.

:::image type="content" source="media/post-meeting-card.png" alt-text="Screenshot of the alternate post-meeting summary card in Teams chat.":::

The card includes:

- **Meeting details**: The meeting title, date, and time.
- **Meeting summary**: AI-generated notes that highlight key discussion points from the meeting.
- **Follow-up and action items**: Actions identified during the meeting that require further attention or completion.
- **Open recap**: A button to open the meeting recap in Teams. In the recap, you can view the recording, transcription, AI-generated notes, and sales-related insights. Learn more about [viewing sales insights in the meeting recap](view-meeting-summary-recap.md#view-sales-related-insights-in-meeting-recap).
